## 1. What Version 1.0 means

Version 1.0 is the formal delivery edition of the report *From China's Solar Supply Chain to U.S. Power Markets* for
the uses stated in its "About this report" page: industry and market screening, procurement diligence and commercial
discussion, comparing contract structures and risk allocation, and conditional capital-recovery analysis. It is not a
valuation of an actual asset, a bankability opinion, a tax-eligibility determination or a price forecast.

The analytical content is that of the reviewed research edition (independent expert and client review of 10 October
2026, verdict "handover-ready for the stated use after the listed minimal edits", applied in the reviewed candidate at
commit 1a3c641), carried through V0.999 (editing and production) and V0.999-R1 (design, structure and three labeled
supplementary calculations). No upstream model was rerun at any of these steps.
