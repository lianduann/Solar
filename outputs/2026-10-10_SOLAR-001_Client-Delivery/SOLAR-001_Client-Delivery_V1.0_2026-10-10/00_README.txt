NOT INCLUDED

Appendix A and Appendix B also mention research working files: source
catalogs, an evidence register and claim register, scenario calculation
notes and files, and a calculation script. These are research working
files and are not part of this package.


SCOPE OF THE REPORT

The report presents conditional scenarios for market screening,
procurement diligence, contract comparison and capital-recovery analysis.
It is not investment advice, a valuation of an actual asset, a financing or
bankability opinion, a tax-credit eligibility determination, or a price
forecast. The 100 MWac Pecos County project analysed in the report is
hypothetical.
